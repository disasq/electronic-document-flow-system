# Planned paired usability study protocol

Status: planned only. No participant outcomes or supervisor approval are recorded here. The targets and interpretation rules are in [the evaluation plan](../09_verification.md).

## Recruitment and consent

After a pilot organisation and its actual baseline process are selected, invite 8–12 volunteers who routinely create, review or track internal documents. Record their roles in anonymised form and disclose any smaller or convenience sample. Explain the purpose, approximate duration, synthetic tasks, voluntary participation, observation and anonymised reporting before the session. Obtain explicit consent to record task outcomes; permit withdrawal without consequence. Keep names, contact details and consent records outside the public repository. Assign participant codes before entering results.

## Session procedure

1. Document the pilot organisation's actual baseline workflow. Use a synthetic folder/spreadsheet/messages rehearsal only when clearly labelled as such.
2. Give the participant equal short training on the baseline and EDFS. Prepare equivalent synthetic document sets; avoid repeating identical content across conditions.
3. Counterbalance order: alternate baseline-first and EDFS-first sessions. Keep the device, browser, network and task count constant where possible.
4. Present four tasks: create and route a request; identify its current status and responsible person; return, revise and resubmit; retrieve the approved version and decision history. Start timing when each task is presented and stop at verified success or five minutes. Record failure or cap rather than excluding it.
5. For each task, record success without help, time, version selection errors, moderator help, failure reason and any observed access or status confusion. Avoid leading hints; record any help given.
6. Debrief with three prompts: what was confusing, which version or status was hardest to identify, and what should change. Record a short anonymised participant statement separately from the observer's interpretation.

## Analysis and records

Use [study_results_template.csv](study_results_template.csv) for task-level data and [test_results_template.csv](test_results_template.csv) for technical checks. Compute each participant's paired time change before taking the median; report completion, version errors, help, capped tasks and all exclusions. Compare the measured outcomes to the **proposed** thresholds in the evaluation plan. Report sample size, missing data, order effects and any baseline differences. Small exploratory samples do not establish population-wide benefit.
