# Demonstration and speaking guide
Suggested duration: 8–10 minutes, adjust to the committee's actual limit.

Ali introduces the proposed departmental problem and states that local validation is still open until real discussions occur. Explain the bounded route and exclusions. Dias presents scientific findings, the four-product comparison and the reason to test configuration before finalising a custom build.

Ali explains role plus ownership policy, immutable versions and a decision tied to one review version. Use context and component diagrams. Dias demonstrates creation, submission, a reviewer returning a document with a reason, revision, resubmission and approval. Ali demonstrates that the other employee cannot find the private draft, then archives as administrator and shows the event history.

State that persona switching is a simulation and that the later backend must enforce identity and transactions. Present the baseline, target metrics and unmeasured status. Show one real YouTrack task and its actual branch, PR, review and check result. Both explain only their actual contributions. Finish by disclosing open interviews, supervisor review and any remaining remote setup actions.

Likely questions: Why not SharePoint or Mayan? Explain the configuration checkpoint. Why not microservices? Explain two-person scope and one transaction boundary. What prevents approving a different version? Explain fixed version ID, revision check, locking and tests. Is this a legal signature? No claim is made. What proves usefulness? Real discussions and a paired baseline study, not prototype screenshots.
